How it started

Comparing a resume with several job descriptions can make it hard to see where to start. This student project was built to bring that comparison into one place and show the skills behind each result.

What you can do today

Paste your resume or upload a PDF or TXT file. The analyzer identifies skill keywords and compares them with sample roles. Open a role to review the skills it asks for, then sign in to save roles you want to revisit.

Our core values

Make results clear

Show which required skills were found so you can understand the match score.

Give people a next step

Highlight gaps so you can decide what to learn or which skills to make clearer on your resume.

Respect resume privacy

Process resume text on the local server and keep it out of the account database.
